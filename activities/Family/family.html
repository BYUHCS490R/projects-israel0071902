The Forger Family

The Forger family looks like a normal family living in the city. However, each member secretly has a unique ability or hidden job. Even with their secrets, they support each other and slowly become a strong and loving family.

Family News

Loid Continues His Mission

Loid works as a psychiatrist during the day while secretly working as a spy called Twilight. He balances work and family carefully. His intelligence helps keep the family safe.

Yor Supports the Family

Yor works at city hall but secretly lives a dangerous life as an assassin called the Thorn Princess. Despite this double life she remains kind and supportive.

Anya Brings Joy

Anya studies at Eden Academy and fills the house with laughter. Her playful personality creates many happy moments for the family. She enjoys spending time with her parents.

Bond Protects the Family

Bond joined the family after being rescued and quickly became a loyal companion. He spends most of his time with Anya and watches over the family carefully. His calm nature makes him an important part of the home.

Interesting Facts

  • Loid is secretly a spy called Twilight.
  • Yor is secretly an assassin called Thorn Princess.
  • Anya can read people's thoughts.
  • Bond can see short visions of the future.
  • The family grows closer while hiding their secrets.
