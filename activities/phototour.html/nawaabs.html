The Nawaab’s

The Nawaab’s offers a slightly more premium dining experience compared to many traditional biryani spots. It serves a variety of Mughlai and Hyderabadi dishes in a comfortable environment. The presentation of food and the quality of service make it a favorite choice for family dinners and special occasions.
