1) The "action" attribute in the form tag tells where the form data will be sent after the user clicks submit.

2) The "method" attribute tells how the form data will be sent to the server, usually using GET or POST.

3) The difference between GET and POST is that GET sends data in the URL and is less secure, while POST sends data in the request body and is more secure.
