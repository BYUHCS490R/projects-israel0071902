Box Model

Engagement

  1. Block-level elements begin on a new line and usually take up the full width of the page. They are used for sections like headings, paragraphs, and divs. Inline elements stay within the same line and only take up the space they need.
  2. The box model includes content, padding, border, and margin. Padding is the space between content and border. Margin is the space outside the border that separates elements.
  3. Common CSS units include px, %, em, rem, vw, and vh. Pixels are fixed units, while percentages and relative units adjust based on screen or text size.
  4. The figure tag is used to group images or diagrams with captions so they remain organized and semantically correct.
