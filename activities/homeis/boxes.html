Box Model

Engagement

  1. Block tags begin on their own line and normally stretch across the full width of the page. They are used for larger parts of a webpage such as headings, paragraphs, and div tags. Inline tags stay within the same line as other content and only use the space they need, like link or span tags.
  2. In the box model, each tag has layers around it. Padding is the space between the content and the border. The border outlines the content area. Margin is the outer space that separates one tag from another.
  3. CSS provides several units for sizing. Pixels (px) give fixed measurements. Percentages (%) adjust based on the parent tag. Units like em and rem depend on text size, while vw and vh adjust according to the browser window size.
  4. A figure tag is used to keep visual content, such as an image or diagram, together with its caption. It helps organize related content in a clear and structured way.
