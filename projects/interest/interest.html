My Interest - Marvel character "DR.Doom"

Interest Hero

DR.Doom

Doctor Doom is a character from Marvel Comics whose real name is Victor Von Doom.He is known for being very intelligent and skilled in science and technology. He wears strong armor that protects him and helps him fight his enemies.

I chose Dr. Doom because he is a powerful thinker and a strong leader. He plans carefully and never gives up, even when things go wrong. Even though he is shown as a villain, his intelligence and confidence make him an interesting character to learn about.
