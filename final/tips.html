Cooking Tips for Students

Students should keep basic ingredients like rice, dal, salt, oil, onions, and spices. These ingredients can be used for many recipes.

Planning meals can save money and time. It is also important to keep the kitchen clean and organized.

Students do not need many tools. A pot, pan, spoon, and knife are enough for many simple dishes.
