Final Project Proposal

1) Title and Topic of My website

Title: Simple and Budget-Friendly Indian Recipes for Students

In this website i will show an easy and affordable Indian recipes including meals, sweets, and drinks for students. The webiste goal is to help students cook simple indian food at their hales.

2) Why I Chose This Topic

I chose this topic because I am from India and I enjoy cooking and eating Indian food. I also know some simple recipes from my hometown. As a student, I understand that students have only limited time or money to cook food. Because of this, I want to share easy and budget-friendly recipes that students can cook without difficulty.
