Simple Indian Sweets

Indian sweets are tasty and special. Many sweets are made during festivals, family events, and celebrations.

Laddu

Laddu is a round Indian sweet often made during festivals and celebrations. It is sweet, soft, and rich.

Time: 30 minutes

Difficulty: Medium

Cost: Medium

Spice: None

Gulab Jamun

Gulab jamun is a famous Indian sweet made from soft dough balls that are fried and soaked in sugar syrup.

Time: 35 minutes

Difficulty: Medium

Cost: Medium

Spice: None

Semiya

Semiya is a sweet dish made with vermicelli, milk, and sugar. It is soft and comforting.

Time: 20 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Cost: Low

Spice: None

Halwa

Halwa is a soft and warm Indian sweet. It is easy to make and has a rich taste because of ghee and sugar.

Time: 20 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Cost: Low

Spice: None
