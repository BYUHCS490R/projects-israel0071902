Refreshing Drinks

These drinks are simple, refreshing, and budget-friendly for students.

Jeera Water

Jeera water is a simple and healthy drink made by boiling cumin seeds in water. It is light and helps with digestion. This drink is often taken in the morning and is easy to prepare at home.

Time: 10 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Cost: Low

Spice: None

Buttermilk

Buttermilk is a cool and refreshing drink made with curd and water. Salt and spices like curry leaves can be added for extra taste. It is very useful in hot weather and helps keep the body cool.

Time: 8 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Cost: Low

Spice: Mild

Lemon Soda

Lemon soda is a refreshing drink made with lemon, soda, salt, and sugar. It is quick to prepare.

Time: 5 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Cost: Low

Spice: None

Lassi

Lassi is a refreshing yogurt-based drink. It is made by mixing curd, water, and sugar or salt. It is smooth, cool, and perfect for hot weather. Lassi is also good for digestion and is commonly served after meals.

Time: 10 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Cost: Low

Spice: None

Sharbath

Sharbath is a sweet and colorful drink made with syrup and water. It is quick to prepare and is very refreshing. It is commonly served during summer and special occasions.

Time: 6 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Cost: Low

Spice: None
